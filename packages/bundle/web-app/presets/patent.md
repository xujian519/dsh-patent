# Patent agent preset

English | [中文](patent.zh.md)

The `patent` agent preset composes a Chinese-patent-engineering agent on the DeepSeek Harness. This bundle declares it in `presets/patent.patch.yml` and ships its sixteen skills under `skills/patent/`. It builds on the `standard` preset and adds the patent domain plugins plus a patent-specific persona and plan-mode discipline, assembled per docs/patent-mode-design.md §4–§9 and plan P4.4 of docs/sati-as-dsh-plugins-plan.md. Law text, examination guidelines, and case decisions are verified preferentially against the local cnlaw REST legal base (semantica-cnlaw, see Prerequisites) with the returned source_path recorded, falling back to patent_case_search / patent_kg_query when it is unavailable. Citation form is checked first against the law index shipped in `@deepseek-ai/dsh-patent-law`, which decides a statute citation against the article text it ships and reports an entry whose text has not been transcribed as unverified rather than accepted. Fee amounts come from `@deepseek-ai/dsh-patent-fees`, which prices the items a case owes from the amounts the package ships and withholds a total only while an applicable item carries none.

## What it mounts

Beyond the standard coding rows a patent workflow needs (shell, filesystem, jobs, reminders, skills, goals and the `/goal` command, plan mode, compaction, delegation, web, and the clock row that tells the model today's date), the preset mounts fourteen further plugins — the patent-domain services and tools plus the literature and methodology rows the patent skills call (assembled in `presets/patent.patch.yml`):

- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-patent-data` — the data seam (ctx.patentData: nuo search provider factory + the ego-browser session runner + the CNIPR channel, which this preset enables through `cniprEnabled` to serve Chinese patent full text and legal status over the ego lite login state on search.cnipr.com). patent_pdf_download runs its ego-browser download adapter through this service and takes China publication numbers through the CNIPR channel; patent_legal_status retries numbers the engine could not answer through it.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-patent-knowledge` — the knowledge.db query service (ctx.patentKnowledge: caseLawSearch / legalSearch / wikiCards / kgSearch / kgGetNode / kgListByType / ipcClassify).
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-patent-workflow` — the execution-pipeline service (ctx.patentWorkflow: runWorkflow / runPlantask / approve / reject).
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-patent-tools` — 36 model-facing tools: search, metadata, legal status, case/wiki/kg queries, law-text search, claim-chart, office-action parsing, TRIZ contradiction analysis, drafting, specification validation, analysis reports, evidence judgment, rule checking, figure analysis and generation, cross-figure consistency, figure comparison, chemical-structure recognition, PDF download, knowledge notes, matter-log archival, workbench case linking, and the workflow/plan state machines (the authoritative list is `docs/tool-catalog.md`).
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-patent-teams` — the durable multi-agent team service (ctx.patentTeams) surfacing the twelve `patent_teams_*` tools; with `qualityGate: true` it runs the composite completion gate, and with `approveCreation` / `approveClosure` on it holds the team's two case-level decisions — creating the team and closing it — to the deployment's `approval` service, refusing the call (outcome `rejected` / `cancelled` / `unavailable`) rather than creating or closing a team the user did not allow.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-patent-rule` — the rule engine, the output gate on tools/post-execute, the declared structural and delivery gates, and the EVI-011 evidence guards.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-patent-document` — render_patent_document.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-patent-filing` — build_patent_filing / verify_patent_filing: assembles one CNIPA application document (abstract, abstract drawing, claims, specification, drawings) into a DOCX whose formatting is reverse-derived from the template shipped in the package, and verifies the finished file against that template.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-patent-deadline` — patent_deadlines: the statutory and designated deadlines of a case, with the 专利法实施细则 period and delivery rules and a shipped holiday calendar for rest-day roll-forward.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-patent-fees` — patent_fees: prices the fee items a case owes against the fee index shipped in that package (application, examination, grant, annual fees, and the procedure fees), counting each item by its basis — per case, per claim beyond the free base, per page, per priority claim, per patent year, per month — and applying the case's fee reduction where the index records one. Amounts ship transcribed from the official fee standard, so the tool returns a total; it withholds one when an applicable item has no amount, which the shipped index still has for the specification surcharge and the extension fee because both are charged in bands the item model cannot hold.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-patent-law` — law_verify: decides each law citation against the law index shipped in that package (《专利法》《专利法实施细则》 by article, 《专利审查指南》 by normalized section path), and reports an indexed entry whose text has not been transcribed as 未核验 instead of accepting it. Both statutes ship transcribed — each entry carries the article text, the document and revision it was copied from, and the copy date — and both article ceilings are verified, so a fabricated number is 超出有效范围 and a valid number the index does not cover is 索引中不存在, neither of them a pass. The guideline ships 63 transcribed sections carrying the text of the 2023 revision, so a guideline citation is decided as 已核验, 与所引命题不符, or 未判定; a row naming only a part, a chapter, or a section, with no section under it transcribed, still reports 未核验. The discipline that a citation must be verified against an official source is unchanged, and the tool no longer lets an unchecked entry read as checked.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-writing-patterns` — query_writing_patterns over the packaged drafting/office-action pattern corpus, plus the system-prompt section carrying the compiled `<writing_skills>` block for claim drafting, specification drafting, and the three office-action categories; the other categories stay reachable through the tool, which searches the whole library.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-tool-literature` — paper_search / paper_list_sources.
- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-methodology` — the `triz` contradiction-matrix lookup tool for explicit improving/worsening parameter pairs. Identifying contradictions and parameter gaps from a disclosure stays with `triz_contradiction_analysis`.

Against the row set `standard` carries, this preset deliberately omits three rows — `tool-plugin-market`, `present`, and `tool-plugin-manager`. Each belongs to the deployment or UI plane rather than to a step of prosecuting a case: deliverables are declared through `render_patent_document` / `build_patent_filing`, which the delivery gate holds to the rule and citation runs and to the round's archival entry (`matter_log_append`), so a second declaration channel would sit outside that gate. The gate also checks the prerequisite results: `rule_check` must report the text it evaluated, a citation-bearing deliverable must come from a `law_verify` run that recognized at least one citation, and an analysis closure must name the manifest its template requires with a successful run. `scripts/preset-mode-baseline.json` records each reason and holds every delivery mode to the shared row set.

The patent services sit behind an isolate realm (patentData / patentKnowledge / patentWorkflow / patentTeams) shared with patent-tools, so its ctx.get('patentData') / ctx.get('patentKnowledge') resolve this preset's instances rather than the host's. tool-ralph is omitted (a patent case uses goal / todo / workflow, not fresh-agent iteration), and tool-web enables fetch because every shipped composition mounts the http fetch provider (`dsh-base` composes `dsh-web-fetch-http` with `fetchProvider: http`, and no bundle disables it), which lets the verify-before-cite rule open a source page instead of trusting a summary.

The preset also mounts `@deepseek-ai/dsh-self-evolve-benchmark` behind its own isolate realm (selfEvolveBenchmark): the benchmark-driven self-evolve provider, programmatic only — no model-facing tool. Its `agentStateDir` points at the seeded `patent-state` work copy under the data root (examples/patent-oas in the package), never the caller's working directory, so a real docket or knowledge base can never be snapshotted or rewritten by an optimize loop.

## Skills

Sixteen skills ship with this preset under `skills/patent/`:

- patent-disclosure-understanding
- patent-prior-art-search
- patent-novelty-inventiveness
- patent-infringement
- patent-invalidity
- patent-invalidation-defense
- patent-oa-response
- patent-reexamination
- patent-quality-gate
- patent-workspace-layout
- patent-team-composition
- inventive-step-analysis
- patent-matter
- patent-fact-check
- patent-compliance-review
- patent-document-polish

`patent-team-composition` is the durable-team template, and the team is opt-in: the persona's work-mode routing makes single-session intent-driven execution the default and admits a team only when the user explicitly asks for one in that turn, so complexity alone is not a reason to build one. Under that instruction this session mounts the `dsh-patent-teams` plugin (`patent_teams_*` tools) and the case picks one of seven scenario role packs covering the full patent lifecycle — case intake (case-manager / researcher / technical-expert / drafter / document-specialist), drafting (researcher / drafter / adversarial-reviewer / technical-expert / applicant-counsel / illustrator / document-specialist), office-action response (same seven), correction (drafter / formal-examiner / illustrator / document-specialist), reexamination (researcher / drafter / adversarial-reviewer / applicant-counsel / illustrator / adjudicator / document-specialist), invalidation (researcher / drafter / technical-expert / invalidity-petitioner / patentee-defender / adjudicator / document-specialist), and infringement litigation (researcher / drafter / technical-expert / patentee-defender / defendant-counsel / adjudicator / document-specialist, plus optional tech-investigator) — led by the current session as captain; the illustrator (`illustrator`) owns the drawings and the numeral table (the sole authority for the rule 21.2 two-way consistency between drawings and description), and the document-specialist (`document-specialist`) owns the scenario-mapped deliverable before closure (correction and beautification only, never substantive conclusions); only when the plugin is disabled does it fall back to single-session `subagent_fork` expert review. In the drafting and office-action packs the captain runs the whole manifest (`patent_disclosure_v1` / `patent_oa_response_v1`) to its approval gate and members read the stage results: `patent_workflow_run` has no per-stage entry point, so a member never claims to have executed a stage such as `claim_strategy`, `draft_claims`, or `tighten`, and a rework is a manifest rerun rather than a hand-written replacement. The reexamination, invalidation, and litigation packs use the adversarial structure of paired positions plus a neutral adjudicator. A team adds human confirmation points rather than removing them: the member roster and task DAG are confirmed with the user before `patent_teams_create`, each stage's key output is shown to the user before the next task starts, strategy choices stay with the user, and the workflow-run closure and the document render are released by the user — the captain never closes a case on its own, and either mode can be switched to the other mid-case after telling the user what is finished and what is still open. Creating the team and closing it are not instructions here but execution points: the row's `approveCreation` / `approveClosure` route `patent_teams_create` and `patent_teams_delete` through the deployment's `approval` service, so an unapproved create or close fails at the call.

`patent-document-polish` is the deliverable-output discipline: pick the scenario template (patentability opinion / search report / OA response / claims-spec / rectification response / re-examination request / invalidation opinion / infringement opinion / litigation pleading), correct (terminology consistency, law-citation format that records the source, numbers/dates/deadlines, numbering levels, legal salutations), and beautify (template rendering, brand injection, A4 layout, md draft → html/pdf or docx), with the delivery release merged into one quality-gate confirmation.

The novelty/inventiveness, infringement, and invalidity skills are rewritten from the Sati skills patent-novelty-analysis, patent-inventiveness-analysis, patent-infringement-checker, and patent-invalidity-checker. Sati tool references (patent_kg_query / patent_case_search / law_search) are replaced by the dsh patent tools, the <memory-context> auto-injection is replaced by explicit must-check lists, and Sati-internal file paths are replaced by workspace-relative paths. The infringement, invalidity, office-action-response, and reexamination skills now drive the real tool faces: claim_chart_build for the element-level chart, parse_office_action for the office-action parse, and patent_workflow_run with patent_infringement_v1 / patent_invalidation_v1 / patent_oa_response_v1 / patent_reexamination_v1 for the deterministic stages, the approval gate, and the run record. `patent-oa-response` and `patent-reexamination` are new here rather than ported.

## Knowledge-base strategy

Per plan P4.4, system knowledge reads dsh-patent-knowledge: case law, wiki cards, and the knowledge graph through patent_case_search / patent_wiki_search / patent_kg_query, and the shipped statute and guideline text through law_search (which selects the statute or guideline collection and returns the article or section text with its source). Law text is verified preferentially against the cnlaw base this deployment declares (see Prerequisites; the endpoints are `patent-law` Config fields) with source_path recorded, and, when the deployment declares no base, through patent_case_search plus web_fetch on authoritative sources. `law_verify` runs before either channel: it fixes the reference form and reports what the shipped index does and does not hold, so a citation that the index cannot support is looked up rather than quoted from memory. The workspace `99-知识库/` directory stays project-level accumulation, recalled with fs-search / grep before going online.

This revises docs/patent-mode-design.md §9, which described a no-engine file library. `99-知识库/` remains project accumulation; the change is that system knowledge now has an engine.

## Prerequisites

The knowledge tools require a knowledge.db. Install one with the patent-knowledge-install bin, or point Config.sourceDbPath at an existing knowledge.db; see packages/patent/patent-knowledge/README.md. Without a database the knowledge tools fail loud at execute time.

The cnlaw legal base is a separate optional enhancement the deployment declares, not a shipped preset: mount the semantica-cnlaw services and set `patent-law`'s Config — `cnlawEnabled` (default `true`), `cnlawSearchUrl` (default `http://127.0.0.1:8100`), `cnlawGraphUrl` (default `http://127.0.0.1:8001`). The plugin renders those values into one system-prompt section, so the persona and the skills point at the declared endpoints instead of naming a port; Neo4j (default 7687) is the base's own store, which the model never reaches directly. With the base declared, law text, examination guidelines, and case decisions are verified through it with source_path recorded; with `cnlawEnabled=false` the declaration states that no base is present and the discipline falls back to patent_case_search / patent_kg_query plus web_fetch. A deployment that additionally mounts the cnlaw MCP bridge (`@deepseek-ai/dsh-mcp-client` over `cnlaw_mcp_launcher.py`) reaches the graph, case, and inventive-step endpoints as `mcp__cnlaw__*` tools, which the persona names first; the semantic-search endpoint and the IPC routes have no tool and stay on REST through curl at the declared endpoints.

Three retrieval skills are user-level, provided by the deployment under `~/.agents/skills/` and not shipped here: `cnipr-search-download` (CNIPR full text and legal status), `cnipa-query` (official CNIPA legal-status transactions), and `google-patents-search` (foreign and global search). The preset's search routine names them as the preferred CN, official-status, and foreign channels, and falls back to the shipped channels whenever one is absent — `patent_search` / `patent_metadata` / `patent_legal_status` over the local nuo engine, `web_search` / `web_fetch`, and `patent_pdf_download` for the original document — with the channels actually used written into the search report.

OpenViking long-term memory is a separate opt-in: the preset ships an `openviking` row with `disabled: true`. A deployment that runs an OpenViking service (see [`@deepseek-ai/dsh-openviking`](../../../memory/openviking/README.md)) duplicates this preset, drops `disabled`, and sets the endpoint and identity headers through `dsh plugin --profile <name> config openviking.endpoint=…` plus `.account` / `.user` / `.agentId` / `.apiKey`; the shipped preset carries no credentials. Recalled text enters the prompt as untrusted background data, so the persona's verify-before-cite discipline still requires source_path from cnlaw or patent_case_search for every legal assertion — OpenViking never replaces the legal base.

## Model Experience

The model sees the Chinese patent-agent persona (professional identity, nine work disciplines, the work-mode routing that defaults to single-session intent-driven execution and admits the team flow only on an explicit user instruction, the standard workflow, the case-type routing table, and the output discipline with its mandatory disclaimer), the patent plan-mode section, the cnlaw declaration section `patent-law` renders from this deployment's Config (the endpoints, or the statement that no base is present), the sixteen preset skills, and the patent tools — including `law_verify`, whose description states that an unverified article is never reported as checked, and `patent_fees`, which prices the fee items a case owes and withholds a total while an applicable item carries no amount — plus the standard coding tools. The persona carries identity, the nine disciplines, and the case-type routing; the channel mechanics, query forms, and citation formats live in the skill each routing line names, so a request pays for them only when a case loads that skill, and `scripts/preset-mode-baseline.json` caps the persona's size so growth has to be a deliberate edit. The persona requires verify-before-cite (web_fetch on every fact), separate comparison, per-feature comparison with citations, and a mandatory disclaimer on every analysis output.

## Known Limitations and Deferred Work

- Legal-text search runs through `law_search`, which retrieves statute and 《专利审查指南》 text from knowledge.db (`ctx.patentKnowledge.legalSearch` / `guidelineSearch`). `law_verify` covers citation form and index membership, not legal text: it decides whether an article is indexed and, once a deployment transcribes an entry, whether the cited proposition matches it. Law text is verified preferentially against the cnlaw base the deployment declares (an optional enhancement whose absence the declaration states, see Prerequisites) — its MCP tools where mounted, REST through curl at the declared endpoints otherwise — and otherwise through patent_case_search plus web_fetch and the `99-知识库/` baseline. Shipped compositions mount the http fetch provider (public HTTP(S) destinations only, each destination resolved, validated, and pinned), so web_fetch works without a deployment change.
- The shipped fee index prices what the item model can express: amounts come from the National Intellectual Property Administration's payment guide (附件2) with the document and copy date recorded per item, the reduction ratios (85% individual, 70% enterprise) are recorded, and a fee whose price differs by patent type is a separate item. Two items still carry no amount — the specification surcharge and the extension fee are charged in bands (by page range, by occurrence) the model has no field for — so a case that reaches either gets no total. The amounts are a mechanical copy of the standard, not a review: no qualified person has read the copy back, so re-check an amount before invoicing.
- The shipped statute text is a mechanical copy of the official revised text, not a review: 已核验 means the article exists and the proposition matches the entry's topics, not that a qualified person read the copy back. Coverage is partial too — 《专利法》 indexes 37 of its 82 articles and 《专利法实施细则》 45 of its 149 — so an existing article the index does not hold reports 索引中不存在 and still has to be looked up. The 《专利审查指南》 sections ship the text of the 2023 revision, but the 2026 revision (in force 2026-01-01) amended the guideline, and the three sections of this index it changed say in their entries that their text predates it. The 《专利法》 topics that arrived as the 2008 revision's numbers 62/69/70 now sit at 67/75/77, where the 2020 text carries them, and 62 carries the compulsory-licence royalty topics its 2020 text supports.
- patent_pdf_download captures the PDF response body in the browser (CDP `Network.getResponseBody`) when the host has a usable ego-browser (ego lite, macOS, on the PATH); when it cannot, it falls back to scraping each page for its CDN PDF link and fetching it over HTTP, so a batch still downloads. A download that fails after both channels reports per-patent errors instead of aborting the batch. knowledge_note_save writes files under the workspace `99-知识库/` directory (a native knowledge.db write API is deferred).
- The 4 rewritten analysis skills inherit Sati's methodology but have not yet been reviewed against current Chinese patent practice; cross-check their checklists against the user's patent-legal baseline before relying on them.
- `patent-oa-response` and `patent-reexamination` are new here, not ported: their step order follows the patent_oa_response_v1 / patent_reexamination_v1 manifests and their deadlines come from patent_deadlines, but neither has been reviewed against the current 审查指南 in practice. The reexamination skill keeps a hit inventiveness ground for a utility model and reports the patent subject separately instead of dropping the ground; that handling is recorded in the workbench plan as still awaiting confirmation.
- `patent-invalidation-defense` is new here, not ported: it answers an invalidation request from the patentee's side, taking its deadline from patent_deadlines through the `invalidation-transfer` notice (a designated period that 专利法实施细则第 75 条 makes non-extendable) and rendering through the `invalidation-defense` template onto the 100902 statement and its 100014 continuation sheet. Like `patent-invalidity` it has not been reviewed against current practice. The guideline sections it names are checked against the shipped index and the cited section's transcribed text (2023 revision); a section the 2026 revision changed still needs that revision's text.
- The design doc's `~/.agents/skills/patent-legal/_shared/patent-law-baseline-2024.md` is a Sati user-level asset not shipped here; the fact-check and quality-gate skills no longer require it, because citation form is checked against the shipped index instead, and law text is verified at use time.
- The self-evolve benchmark is programmatic only: `ctx.selfEvolveBenchmark` mounts no model-facing tool; establish-baseline / optimize loops run from an operator or script that resolves the service for an agent. Its default seams fork children over the host subagents registry, so a child inherits this preset's approval setting (`'never'`) and plan-mode discipline — approval-gated operations are refused in children, and the executor prompt explicitly exits plan semantics so a deliverable can be produced directly.
